Questions

The short answers.

What is a Founding Athlete?

A creator from our founding group who runs their own community on WHAM. You get the community, the tools to run it, the Founding badge and WHAM Pro.

Is it free?

Yes. There's nothing to pay, to apply or to take part.

Is it paid?

No. This isn't a paid or affiliate programme, and there's no commission. What you get is WHAM Pro and your community.

Do I need a large following?

No. We care more about whether people genuinely train with you than about the number.

Do I have to use WHAM already?

No, though you'll need an account before your community goes live.

How do you select people?

We read each application ourselves and look for real passion for training and a community that would use it.

What happens after I apply?

We email you with the answer. If you're selected, we set up your community, give you your link and send you a short list of first steps.
