Billboards

Each ad shows on every WHAM phone, signed in or not, within about 10 minutes (next time the app comes back to the front). The art is drawn contain-fit — nothing is cropped or stretched. On the building sign the spare space is dark; on the three wall billboards it's see-through (the night sky / wall shows, and a transparent PNG floats like WHAM's own signs). Either way a logo on black at the slot's shape looks best. Tapping an ad with a link asks “Visit <name>?” before opening it.

JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 8 MB. Around 1200–1600 px wide is plenty — phones decode the whole image, so bigger only costs memory.
